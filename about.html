Our Story

The Gorda behind the Goodies.

Where It Began

A love of Peru, baked into every batch.

Gorda's Goodies grew out of a simple thing: a family love of Peruvian alfajores, and a belief that they're better homemade. What started as baking for family and friends turned into Gorda's Goodies — a small, home-based bakery bringing a taste of Peru to Northern Virginia.

Every alfajor starts with real manjar blanco, made from scratch and filled by hand between two layers of buttery shortbread. Nothing comes out of a jar, and nothing gets rushed — these are the same recipes and the same care you'd find in a Peruvian home kitchen.

What Makes Them Different

Handmade, small-batch, made to order

🧑‍🍳 Made by hand

No factory lines here — every cookie is shaped, filled, and finished by hand, in small batches.

🍯 Real manjar blanco

Our dulce de leche filling is made from scratch, not store-bought — it's the heart of every alfajor.

📦 Fresh to order

We bake to order for pickup, gifting, and events — nothing sits around waiting on a shelf.

Custom Orders & Events

We cater weddings, showers, and every occasion in between.

Beyond everyday orders, Gorda's Goodies puts together custom dessert tables and platters for weddings, graduations, holidays, baby showers, and team celebrations across Springfield, Herndon, and the wider Northern Virginia area.
