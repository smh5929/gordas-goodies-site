How To Order

No online checkout — here's how it works

1. Reach out

Send us your order and pickup date by phone, email, or Instagram DM — at least 48 hours ahead.

2. We confirm your total

We'll text or email back to confirm availability, pickup time, and what you owe.

3. Pay via Venmo or Zelle

Your order is confirmed once payment is received. See the footer below for our Venmo and Zelle info.
